Para sa inilagay na sintomas, magbibigay ang gabay ng pangkalahatang hakbang lamang—hindi diagnosis.For a custom symptom, the guide gives general safety steps only—not a diagnosis.

Laging sundin ang planong ibinigay ng iyong dialysis teamFollow the plan from your dialysis team

Huwag baguhin ang gamot, iron/epoetin, fluid limit, pagkain, o dialysis schedule batay lamang sa gabay na ito. Ibahagi agad sa care team ang abnormal o critical na resulta at anumang bago o lumalalang sintomas.Do not change medicines, iron/epoetin, fluid limits, diet, or dialysis schedule based only on this guide. Promptly share abnormal or critical results and any new or worsening symptoms with your care team.
