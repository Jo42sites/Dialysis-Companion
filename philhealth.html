PhilHealth at tulongPhilHealth and assistance

Malaki ang maitutulong ng PhilHealth, pero hindi nito sinasagot ang lahat. Alamin ang package, limit, at iba pang tulong bago ang krisis. PhilHealth can help a lot, but it does not cover every cost. Learn the package, limits, and other assistance before a crisis.

PaalalaNote

Ang package ay ibinabawas sa bill ng accredited facility, hindi cash na ibinibigay sa pasyente. Maaaring may iba pang singil na hindi sakop. Itanong sa billing office bago ang treatment.The package is deducted from the accredited facility’s bill, not paid as cash to the patient. Other charges may not be covered. Ask the billing office before treatment.

PD at transplant packages / PD and transplant packages

Ang peritoneal dialysis ay may Z benefit packages batay sa bags bawat araw: halos ₱389,640 para sa 3 bags/day at ₱510,140 para sa 4 bags/day, kasama ang ancillary services. Ang kidney transplant package ay nag-iiba ayon sa donor at surgery at umaabot sa milyon. Kailangan ng pre-authorization at accredited center.Peritoneal dialysis has Z benefit packages based on bags per day: about ₱389,640 for 3 bags/day and ₱510,140 for 4 bags/day, including ancillary services. Kidney transplant packages vary by donor and surgery and reach millions. Pre-authorization and an accredited center are required.

Pangkalahatang impormasyon lamang. I-verify ang eksaktong halaga at eligibility sa PhilHealth at sa PD/transplant center.General information only. Verify exact amounts and eligibility with PhilHealth and the PD/transplant center.

Bawal ang fixerNo fixers

Walang bayad ang pagpapa-process ng PhilHealth. Huwag magbigay ng pera sa nag-aalok ng “mabilis na approval.” Sa opisyal na billing o Malasakit Center magtanong.PhilHealth processing is free. Do not pay anyone who offers “fast approval.” Ask the official billing office or Malasakit Center.
