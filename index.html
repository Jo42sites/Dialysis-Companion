Simula ritoStart here

Ano ang dialysis?What is dialysis?

Kapag hindi na makapaglinis ang bato, dumarami ang waste at tubig sa katawan. Tinutulungan ng dialysis na alisin ang ilan sa mga ito. Hindi ito lunas, pero nakakapagbigay ng mas mahabang buhay at mas magandang pakiramdam. When the kidneys can no longer clean the blood, waste and extra fluid build up. Dialysis helps remove some of it. It is not a cure, but it can help people live longer and feel better.

HD sa centerIn-center HD

2–3× linggo, 3–4 oras. Kailangan ng permanenteng access at biyahe.2–3× a week, 3–4 hours. Needs permanent access and travel.

HD sa bahayHome HD

Mas madalas pero mas maikli. Kailangan ng training, espasyo, at kasama.More frequent but shorter. Needs training, space, and a care partner.

Peritoneal dialysisPeritoneal dialysis

Gamit ang lining ng tiyan. Mas flexible ang oras; kailangan ng malinis na lugar at disiplina.Uses the abdominal lining. More flexible timing; needs a clean space and discipline.

Kidney transplantKidney transplant

Pinakamalapit sa normal na function. Kailangan ng donor, surgery, at lifetime na gamot.Closest to normal function. Needs a donor, surgery, and lifelong medicines.

Mahalagang tandaanRemember

Bawat session ay mahalaga. Ang pagliban o pagpaikli ay parang pag-skip ng gamot — puwedeng magdulot ng mataas na potassium, tubig sa baga, at pagod. Kung may problema sa schedule, sabihin agad sa unit; huwag basta mag-skip.Every session matters. Missing or shortening one is like skipping medicine — it can cause high potassium, fluid in the lungs, and fatigue. If there is a schedule problem, tell your unit; do not simply skip.

Para sa ibang pasyente, ang conservative kidney management (sintomas at kalidad ng buhay nang walang dialysis) ay lehitimong pagpipilian. Pag-usapan ito nang tapat sa doktor at pamilya.For some patients, conservative kidney management (symptoms and quality of life without dialysis) is a valid choice. Discuss it honestly with your doctor and family.

Hakbang-hakbangStep by step

Ang araw ng dialysisYour dialysis day

Apat na bahagi lang ang kabisaduhin. Kapag alam mo ang susunod, mas kalmado ka at mas nakakapagtanong. Just remember four parts. Knowing what comes next helps you stay calmer and ask better questions.

1

Bisperas ng gabiThe night before

Ihanda ang bag, gamot, ID, at PhilHealth. Sundin ang fluid limit. Tingnan ang access bago matulog.Pack your bag, medicines, ID, and PhilHealth. Follow your fluid limit. Check your access before sleeping.

2

Umaga bago umalisMorning before leaving

Inumin lamang ang pinayagang gamot. Magsuot ng maluwag at magdala ng jacket. Huwag kumain ng mabigat bago ang session.Take only allowed medicines. Wear loose clothing and bring a jacket. Avoid a heavy meal before the session.

3

Sa unit at habang nagdadialysisAt the unit and during dialysis

Pagdating sa center, magtimbang agad bago ang dialysis. Ipaalam agad ang lagnat, ubo, pagdurugo, o hindi pag-inom ng gamot. Habang nakasaksak, sabihin agad ang hilo, pulikat, pagsusuka, o sakit ng dibdib.On arrival at the center, weigh in before dialysis. Report fever, cough, bleeding, or missed medicines right away. During treatment, report dizziness, cramps, vomiting, or chest pain immediately.

4

Pagkatapos at pag-uwiAfter and at home

Pagkatapos ng dialysis, magtimbang muli bago umalis at itala. Magpahinga bago tumayo. I-check ang bleeding at access. Iwasan ang mabigat na buhat gamit ang access arm.After dialysis, weigh again before leaving and record it. Rest before standing. Check bleeding and access. Avoid heavy lifting with the access arm.

Ang iyong lifelineYour lifeline

Pag-aalaga ng accessCaring for your access

Ang access ay ang daan para sa dialysis. Araw-araw itong tingnan, damhin, at alagaan. Ang maagang pag-aksiyon ay nakakapagligtas ng access at buhay. Your access is the doorway for dialysis. Check, feel, and protect it every day. Acting early can save both the access and your life.

TingnanLook

Pamumula, pamamaga, pasa, o sugat.Redness, swelling, bruising, or wounds.

DamhinFeel

Ang “thrill” o pagaspas sa fistula.The “thrill” or buzzing over the fistula.

PakingganListen

Ang “bruit” o ugong gamit ang stethoscope.The “bruit” or whooshing with a stethoscope.

Huwag gawin sa access armDo not do these on the access arm

  • BP cuff, IV, blood extraction, o injection.BP cuff, IV, blood draw, or injection.
  • Mabigat na buhat, tight na relo, o pulseras.Heavy lifting, tight watches, or bracelets.
  • Pag tulog nang nakadagan ang braso.Sleeping on the access arm.

Ipaalam sa lahat ng health worker na may fistula ka bago sila mag-BP o mag-inject.Tell every health worker about your fistula before they take BP or give an injection.

Kapag may problemaIf something is wrong

  • Pagdurugo: idiin nang 10–15 minuto, iangat ang braso, tumawag sa unit.Bleeding: apply pressure for 10–15 minutes, raise the arm, call the unit.
  • Nawawalang thrill/bruit, malamig at masakit na braso: ER agad.Missing thrill/bruit, cold and painful arm: go to the ER now.

Pang-araw-araw na alagaEveryday care

  • Panatilihing tuyo at malinis ang dressing; huwag buksan o hilahin.Keep the dressing dry and clean; do not open or pull it.
  • I-secure ang tubing at tingnan ang exit site araw-araw.Secure the tubing and check the exit site daily.
  • Huwag lumangoy o magbabad nang walang pahintulot.Do not swim or soak without permission.

Kapag may lagnat o aksidenteIf there is fever or an accident

  • Lagnat o panginginig na may catheter: tumawag agad sa unit o ER.Fever or chills with a catheter: call the unit or ER right away.
  • Kung mabunot: idiinan ng malinis na gauze, tumawag, at huwag ibalik.If it comes out: apply clean gauze pressure, call, and do not push it back.

Ligtas na PD exchangeSafe PD exchange

  • Maghugas ng kamay at magsuot ng mask; linisin ang lugar.Wash hands, wear a mask, and clean the area.
  • Check ang bag: expiration, butas, kalinawan, at tamang fluid.Check the bag: expiration, leaks, clarity, and correct fluid.
  • Huwag hawakan ang dulo ng tubing; panatilihing closed ang system.Do not touch the tubing ends; keep the system closed.

Senyales ng peritonitisSigns of peritonitis

Malabong drain fluid, sakit ng tiyan, lagnat, o pagsusuka. Tumawag agad sa PD unit at itago ang unang malabong bag. Huwag magdagdag ng antibiotic nang walang utos.Cloudy drain fluid, abdominal pain, fever, or vomiting. Call the PD unit immediately and keep the first cloudy bag. Do not add antibiotics without instruction.

Pagkain at tubigFood and fluid

Walang iisang diet para sa lahat. Depende ang payo sa labs, timbang, gamot, at uri ng dialysis. Layunin: sapat na sustansya, ligtas na potassium at phosphorus, at kontroladong asin at likido. There is no single diet for everyone. Advice depends on labs, weight, medicines, and dialysis type. The goal: enough nutrition, safe potassium and phosphorus, and controlled salt and fluid.

  • ProteinIsda, itlog, manok; huwag bawasan nang wala sa payo.Fish, egg, chicken; do not cut back without advice.
  • GulayVegetablesBanlawan at pakuluan; itapon ang tubig.Rinse and boil; discard the water.
  • KaninRiceSukatin ang serving ayon sa plano.Measure the portion per your plan.
  • Prutas at tubigFruit and fluidMaliit na serving; bilangin ang likido.Small serving; count the fluid.

Halimbawa lamang ito. Sundin ang plano ng inyong dietitian.This is only an example. Follow your dietitian’s plan.

Sodium

Limitahan ang tuyo, patis, at processed food. Nagpapataas ng BP at uhaw.Limit dried fish, fish sauce, and processed food. Raises BP and thirst.

Potassium

Depende sa lab. Iwasan ang salt substitute at sobrang sabaw ng gulay.Depends on labs. Avoid salt substitutes and excess vegetable broth.

Phosphorus

Iwasan ang cola at processed meat. Inumin ang binder kasabay ng pagkain.Avoid cola and processed meat. Take binders with meals.

Protein

Huwag magbawas nang wala sa payo; kailangan ito sa lakas at laban sa infection.Do not cut back without advice; it supports strength and infection defense.

High K High P High Na Protein Fluid

I-scroll ang listahan o mag-search.Scroll the list or search.

Ang “high” ay pangkalahatang babala, hindi eksaktong sukat. Ang serving, paraan ng pagluto, at latest labs ang pagbatayan. Tanungin ang dietitian bago magbawas ng pagkain.“High” is a general warning, not an exact measure. Serving size, cooking method, and latest labs matter. Ask your dietitian before removing foods.

BabalaWarning

Huwag gumamit ng “salt substitute” o “low-sodium salt” — kadalasan ito ay mataas sa potassium. Iwasan din ang herbal detox at kidney cleanse.Do not use “salt substitute” or “low-sodium salt” — these are often high in potassium. Also avoid herbal detox and kidney cleanses.

Gamot at numeroMedicines and numbers

Gamot at labsMedicines and labs

Maraming gamot ang may espesyal na dosis kapag may kidney failure. Huwag mag-adjust nang sarili. Dalhin ang lahat ng gamot at supplement sa bawat check-up. Many medicines need special dosing in kidney failure. Do not adjust on your own. Bring all medicines and supplements to every check-up.

Phosphate bindersPhosphate binders

Kasabay ng pagkain o meryenda. Walang pagkain, kadalasang walang bisa.Take with meals or snacks. Without food, they usually do not work.

BP at pusoBP and heart

Ipaalam ang hilo o mababang BP. May gamot na hinohold bago ang dialysis.Report dizziness or low BP. Some medicines are held before dialysis.

AnemiaAnemia

EPO at iron ayon sa reseta; kailangan ng CBC at iron tests.EPO and iron as prescribed; CBC and iron tests are needed.

Vitamin D at butoVitamin D and bone

Para sa calcium, phosphorus, at PTH. Huwag magdagdag nang wala sa payo.For calcium, phosphorus, and PTH. Do not add extra without advice.

Babala sa gamotMedicine warning

Iwasan ang NSAIDs gaya ng ibuprofen at mefenamic acid maliban kung sinabi ng doktor. Huwag uminom ng herbal, “kidney cleanse,” o gamot ng kaibigan. Ipaalam ang lahat ng gamot at allergy.Avoid NSAIDs such as ibuprofen and mefenamic acid unless your doctor says otherwise. Do not take herbal products, “kidney cleanses,” or a friend’s medicine. Report all medicines and allergies.

Kailan ang mga labs? / When are labs done?
  • Bawat session: timbang pagdating bago ang dialysis, BP, at pagtingin sa access.Every session: arrival weight before dialysis, BP, and access check.
  • Buwanan: CBC, potassium, phosphorus, calcium, albumin, at BUN/creatinine ayon sa unit.Monthly: CBC, potassium, phosphorus, calcium, albumin, and BUN/creatinine per unit.
  • Bawat 3–6 buwan: PTH, iron studies, at Kt/V/URR.Every 3–6 months: PTH, iron studies, and Kt/V/URR.
  • Taunan: hepatitis screening, vaccine review, at transplant discussion.Yearly: hepatitis screening, vaccine review, and transplant discussion.

Hindi nag-iisaYou are not alone

Pamilya at care teamFamily and care team

Malaking bahagi ng pag-aalaga ay nangyayari sa bahay. Mahalaga rin ang pahinga ng caregiver at malinaw na komunikasyon sa team. Much of care happens at home. Caregiver rest and clear communication with the team matter too.

Araw-araw sa bahayEveryday at home

  • Ipaalala ang tamang gamot sa tamang oras.Remind the patient about the right medicine at the right time.
  • Itala ang timbang pagdating at pagkatapos, BP, at nainom na likido.Record arrival and post-dialysis weight, BP, and fluid intake.
  • Tingnan ang access at magtanong kung may pagbabago.Check the access and ask about any change.
  • Ihanda ang pagkain ayon sa plano; iwasan ang hilaw na isda o karne.Prepare food per the plan; avoid raw fish or meat.
  • Ipaalam agad ang sintomas; huwag itago dahil baka maudlot ang session.Report symptoms early; do not hide them for fear of missing a session.

Kumuha ng backup caregiver at pahinga. Ang pagod at inis ay normal; magsabi kapag sobra na.Arrange a backup caregiver and rest. Fatigue and frustration are normal; speak up when it is too much.

Huwag gawin kahit may mabuting intensyonDo not do these, even with good intentions

  • Huwag magbigay ng herbal o “detox” na hindi sinangguni.Do not give unapproved herbal or “detox” products.
  • Huwag baguhin ang gamot, dosis, o fluid limit.Do not change medicines, doses, or the fluid limit.
  • Huwag pakainin ng bawal na pagkain nang palihim.Do not secretly feed restricted foods.
  • Huwag ipagkait ang dialysis bilang parusa o panakot.Do not withhold dialysis as punishment or threat.
  • Huwag sabihing “arte lang” ang sintomas.Do not dismiss symptoms as “just acting.”

Mabilisang sagotQuick answers

FAQFAQ

Madalas itanong ng pasyente at pamilya. Pangkalahatan ang bawat sagot; ang care team pa rin ang magsasabi kung ano ang tama para sa inyo. Questions patients and families often ask. Each answer is general; your care team still decides what is right for you.

Mabubuhay ba ako nang normal? / Can I live a normal life?

Maraming pasyente ang nakakapagtrabaho at nakakasama sa pamilya. Kailangan ng schedule, disiplina sa gamot at fluid, at suporta. Iba-iba ang “normal” para sa bawat tao.Many patients work and spend time with family. It takes schedule, medicine and fluid discipline, and support. “Normal” is different for each person.

Masakit ba ang dialysis? / Is dialysis painful?

Ang needle insertion ay maaaring kirot sandali. Ang mismong dialysis ay hindi dapat masakit. Sabihin sa nurse ang pulikat, sakit ng ulo, o discomfort.Needle insertion may pinch briefly. Dialysis itself should not hurt. Tell the nurse about cramps, headache, or discomfort.

Pwede ba akong kumain ng saging? / Can I eat bananas?

Depende sa potassium mo at serving size. Mataas sa potassium ang saging. Itanong sa dietitian kung gaano karami at gaano kadalas.It depends on your potassium and serving size. Bananas are high in potassium. Ask your dietitian how much and how often.

Pwede ba akong uminom ng kape? / Can I drink coffee?

Kape ay likido at may potassium; ang creamer at asukal ay may phosphorus at calories. Kung papayagan, maliit na serving at bilangin sa limit. Limitahan ang 3-in-1 kung mataas ang phosphorus.Coffee is fluid and has potassium; creamer and sugar add phosphorus and calories. If allowed, keep it small and count it in your limit. Limit 3-in-1 mixes if phosphorus is high.

Pwede ba akong magtrabaho o maglakbay? / Can I work or travel?

Marami ang nakakapagtrabaho at nakakapaglakbay nang planado. Kailangan ng flexible schedule at pag-iwas sa mabigat na buhat sa access arm. Para sa biyahe, magtanong sa unit 4–6 linggo bago at mag-book ng dialysis slot sa destinasyon.Many people work and travel with planning. It needs a flexible schedule and avoiding heavy lifting with the access arm. For travel, ask your unit 4–6 weeks ahead and book a dialysis slot at your destination.

May gamot ba para gumaling ang bato? / Is there a cure?

Sa advanced kidney failure, wala pang gamot na nagbabalik ng normal na bato. Dialysis, transplant, at conservative care ang pangunahing opsyon. Mag-ingat sa nag-aalok ng “lunas” o “kidney cleanse.”In advanced kidney failure, no medicine restores normal kidneys. Dialysis, transplant, and conservative care are the main options. Beware of “cures” or “kidney cleanses.”
