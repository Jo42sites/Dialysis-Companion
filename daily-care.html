Gabay sa dialysisDialysis guide

Pagkain at tubigFood and fluid

Hindi ito kapalit ng payo ng nephrologist o dialysis team. Ang itinatalagang diet ay depende sa labs, timbang, gamot at uri ng dialysis.This does not replace advice from your nephrologist or dialysis team. The prescribed diet depends on labs, weight, medicines and dialysis type.

Simpleng plate checkA simple plate check

Halimbawa lamang ito. Sundin ang plano ng inyong dietitian.This is only an example. Follow your dietitian’s plan.

Sodium

Limitahan ang asin, patis, at tuyo.Limit salt, fish sauce, dried fish.

Potassium

Depende sa lab.Depends on your labs.

Phosphorus

Iwasan ang cola; binder sa kain.Avoid cola; binder with meals.

Protein

Huwag bawasan nang wala sa payo.Do not cut back unprescribed.

Suriin ang pagkainCheck a food

Mas mabuting sundin ang personal na plano ng renal dietitian.Follow your renal dietitian’s personal plan.

    Hanapin ang pagkain — listahanFind a food — list
    High K High P High NA Protein Fluid

    I-scroll ang listahan o mag-search.Scroll the list or search.

    Ang “high” ay pangkalahatang babala, hindi eksaktong sukat. Ang serving, paraan ng pagluto, at latest labs ang pagbatayan. Tanungin ang dietitian bago magbawas ng pagkain.“High” is a general warning, not an exact measure. Serving size, cooking method, and latest labs matter. Ask your dietitian before removing foods.

    IwasanAvoid NSAIDs (ibuprofen, mefenamic acid) · salt substitute · herbal detoxNSAIDs (ibuprofen, mefenamic acid) · salt substitutes · herbal detox
