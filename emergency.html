Emergency at sintomasEmergencies and symptoms

Pula: 911 / ER ngayonRed: 911 / ER now

  • Hirap huminga sa pahinga o hindi makahigaTrouble breathing at rest or cannot lie flat
  • Sakit ng dibdib o mabilis/iregular na tibokChest pain or fast/irregular heartbeat
  • Nahimatay, seizure, o pagkalitoFainting, seizure, or confusion
  • Pagdurugong ayaw tumigil sa accessBleeding from the access that will not stop
  • Lagnat/panginginig na may catheterFever/chills with a catheter
  • Malabong PD fluid at sakit ng tiyanCloudy PD fluid with abdominal pain

Dilaw: ipaalam ngayonAmber: tell the team today

  • Nahihilo, pawis, o mababang BPDizziness, sweating, or low BP
  • Pulikat o paulit-ulit na pagsusukaCramps or repeated vomiting
  • Pamamaga ng binti o mukhaLeg or face swelling
  • Biglaang pagtaba ng timbangSudden weight gain

Berde: sa susunod na check-upGreen: next check-up

  • Banayad na pangangati o dry skinMild itching or dry skin
  • Constipation o hirap matulogConstipation or trouble sleeping
  • Mga tanong sa gamot o resultaQuestions about medicines or results

Kung lumala o may bagong sintomas, huwag hintayin ang check-up.If it worsens or a new symptom appears, do not wait for the check-up.

Ilarawan ang emergency o sintomasDescribe the emergency or symptom

I-type ang nararamdaman at tingnan kung anong antas ng tulong ang dapat hingin. Kung may matinding sintomas, huwag maghintay sa checker—tumawag sa 911.Type what is happening to see what level of help to seek. For severe symptoms, do not wait for this checker—call 911.

Huwag maglagay ng pangalan o personal na impormasyon. Hanggang 300 character.Do not include names or personal information. Up to 300 characters.

Tumawag sa 911Call 911

Awtomatikong pagtukoy lamang ito, hindi diagnosis. Maaaring hindi makilala ang lahat ng salita. Kung hindi sigurado, lumalala, o hindi pangkaraniwan ang sintomas, makipag-ugnayan agad sa healthcare worker; kung matindi o may panganib sa buhay, tumawag sa 911.This is automated keyword guidance, not a diagnosis. It may not recognize every word. If unsure, worsening, or unusual, contact a healthcare worker promptly; for severe or life-threatening symptoms, call 911.
